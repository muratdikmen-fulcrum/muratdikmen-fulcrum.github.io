MD-Assistant

A private personal assistant, used by one person: its author.

MD-Assistant is a self-hosted application that reads its owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar to summarise what needs attention and answer questions about it. It runs on a single private server and is not offered to anyone else.

Who can use it

Nobody but the account owner. There is no sign-up, no shared instance, and no public interface. The Google OAuth consent screen exists only so the owner can grant the application access to their own data.

What it accesses

Read-only access to the owner's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events. It does not send mail, modify messages, or change calendar entries.

Privacy
