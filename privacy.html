Privacy Policy

MD-Assistant · last updated 12 September 2026

MD-Assistant is a self-hosted personal assistant with exactly one user: its author and operator. It is not a service offered to other people, and it holds no data belonging to anyone other than its operator.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail — message content and metadata, read-only (gmail.readonly).
  • Google Calendar — event details, read-only (calendar.readonly).

It does not request permission to send, modify, or delete anything.

Where the data goes

Data is processed on a single private server controlled by the operator. Message and calendar content may be sent to third-party language-model providers in order to summarise it, reached through OpenRouter. Those providers process the content to produce a response and are bound by their own terms. No other third party receives the data.

What is not done with it

  • It is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising.
  • It is not used to train any model, and provider settings are configured to opt out of training where that option exists.
  • It is not made available to any other person.

Retention

Working copies of message and calendar data are held only as long as needed to produce a summary and are then deleted on a fixed schedule. Operational audit records, which describe what the system did rather than the contents of messages, are retained for a limited period for troubleshooting.

Access, correction and deletion

The only user is the operator, who can revoke the application's access at any time at Google Account permissions, and can delete all stored data directly on the server.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the contact address shown on the Google OAuth consent screen for this application.
